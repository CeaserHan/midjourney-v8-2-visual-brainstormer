**视觉发现：** 不靠降雨本身，而用湿润表面、滴水边缘与排水痕迹证明阵雨刚刚结束。

**构图作用：** 低位近景放大水迹，中景展开城市表面，远景只保留可读的香港空间密度。

A low observation height enlarges the wet foreground evidence before the broader urban environment unfolds beyond, with credible period construction, signage systems and accumulated everyday wear. Naturalistic on-location appearance shaped by Hong Kong New Wave urban realism, unforced spatial relationships and tactile post-rain detail.

## 7–12 大胆发散

### 7. 倒影将城市拆成多个版本

**视觉发现：** 雨后既有湿表面将建筑边缘和模拟信息拆成不连续的第二城市。

**构图作用：** 高位斜俯视同时看见实体结构和湿润平面，反射占据足够面积但不取代现实空间。

```text
Live-action cinematic still for a city-image magazine report, Hong Kong in 1980 at early night after a humid summer shower, where existing wet surfaces break structural edges, analog signs and dense urban fragments into a discontinuous reflected city. A high diagonal observation keeps the physical environment readable in the upper spatial layers while reflections occupy a substantial lower plane, fragmented by drainage lines, surface texture and remaining raindrops rather than becoming a perfect mirror. Credible period reconstruction, naturalistic material behavior and Hong Kong New Wave urban realism.
```

**视觉发现：** 水流不再只是表面效果，它沿着坡度、接缝与台阶显示平时不易察觉的城市微地形。

**构图作用：** 接近地面的俯视让多条水路从近景分叉到中景，结构边缘只作为坡度证据。

```text
Live-action cinematic still for a city-image magazine report, Hong Kong in 1980 at early night immediately after a humid summer shower, with shallow runoff tracing hidden changes in urban slope, seams, steps and joined surfaces. Viewed close to the ground with a downward angle, several water paths divide from the near foreground into the middle distance, revealing subtle topography through flow direction and accumulated moisture while period structural edges provide scale and context. Naturalistic physical behavior, credible 1980 Hong Kong construction and Hong Kong New Wave attention to overlooked city mechanisms.
```
